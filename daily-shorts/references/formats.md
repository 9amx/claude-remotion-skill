# Formats (rotate them)

Six proven Short structures. Rotation keeps the channel from looking mass-produced (YouTube
limits reach and monetisation for repetitive templated content) and tells us, through
`yt-stats.mjs`, which ones this audience watches. Never the same format two days in a row.
Record the format in `project.daily.format`.

| Format | Use when | Structure (about 50 s) | Opening visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| `breaking` | it happened in the last 72 h | the event in one line -> where/when -> what is known -> what is not known yet -> what happens next | globe flying to the place |
| `minute-by-minute` | there is an official timeline (ATC audio, NTSB preliminary report) | a clock counts down: "9:03 p.m. ... 9:05 ... 9:06" -> the last known moment -> what investigators found | timeline graphic or globe route |
| `mystery` | missing aircraft, unexplained events, open investigations | the unanswered question first -> the facts in order -> the clues -> the question again (loop) | globe with the last known position pulsing |
| `what-went-wrong` | the final report is out (NTSB/AAIB/BEA) | the outcome -> the chain of 3 causes the report names -> the rule that changed | statement graphic or the aircraft |
| `on-this-day` | an anniversary this week | "X years ago today..." -> the story -> why it still matters | archival photo (public domain) |
| `by-the-numbers` | storms, quakes, fires, records | a staggering number -> 3 more numbers that build -> what it means for people | counter graphic |

Rules for every format:
- The first 1.5 s answers "why should I watch?" with picture, words and on-screen text together.
- A new piece of information or a visual change every 2-3 seconds.
- The last line makes the first line land again (loop) or asks a question people answer in comments.
- No spoken "like and subscribe". A good loop and a strong next video do more.
